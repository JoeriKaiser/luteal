Luteal is a menstrual cycle tracker built around a simple rule: what you recorded and what the app calculated are never presented as the same thing.

Recorded observations are labelled as recorded. Predictions are labelled as estimates, shown as a range rather than a single confident date, and the app tells you what that range was derived from. Luteal makes no diagnosis and draws no medical conclusions.

OFFLINE BY DEFAULT

Everything is stored in a local database on your device. No account is needed and nothing is sent anywhere until you explicitly turn sync on. Android's cloud backup is disabled for the app's data, so your tracking history does not end up in a Google backup.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK

- Cycle starts, and bleeding from spotting through to heavy
- Pain, mood and energy on a 1-5 scale
- Cramps, headache, fatigue, bloating, breast tenderness, mood changes, skin and pelvic pain
- Private notes, which are never included in any sharing
- Past cycles, so estimates become useful sooner

ESTIMATES YOU CAN REASON ABOUT

The estimated range for your next period widens or narrows based on your own recorded history. You can optionally declare areas of focus (PMS, PMDD, endometriosis, PCOS, perimenopause, thyroid) and an age range; these only widen the range or suggest extra observations, and they stay on your device.

Alongside your data, Luteal shows sourced facts about menstrual cycles, each linked to its published reference, because a tracker that only ever shows a 28-day average teaches people something false.

DUO SHARING, IF YOU WANT IT

You can link with a partner and choose, category by category, what they see: cycle day, the estimated range, mood, energy, support requests. Every category is independent and revocable at any time. Private notes are never shareable.

OPTIONAL SYNC

Sync across your devices is off by default. When enabled, your records are end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload: the server stores only ciphertext it cannot read. Your account code is the only key, and losing it means the synced data cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.

The sync backend is currently invitation-only, so a code is needed to create an account. The app is fully usable offline without one.

NO TRACKING

No advertising, no analytics, no Google Play Services, no Firebase. Crash reports are opt-in per crash: nothing is sent unless you read the dialog and choose to send it, and your tracking data is not included.
