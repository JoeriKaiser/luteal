# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Luteal serves two equally important participants:

- People privately recording and understanding their own menstrual cycles, including regular and irregular experiences.
- Their chosen Duo partners, who receive only the information explicitly shared with them and use it to offer informed, respectful support.

The primary tracker needs fast, low-friction daily logging, a trustworthy cycle history, understandable estimates, and complete control over sensitive information. The Duo partner needs a purpose-built experience rather than a reduced copy of the tracker interface.

## Product Purpose

Luteal is a French-first, offline-first cycle tracker and consensual Duo companion. It helps people record periods, symptoms, observations, and personal patterns over time while making uncertainty visible and preserving user control.

Luteal records and presents user-entered information. It does not diagnose conditions, make medical claims, or represent estimates as clinical conclusions. Success means that both solo and Duo workflows feel useful from the first release, sensitive data remains private by default, and every shared detail is intentional and reversible.

## Brand Personality

Reassuring, discreet, precise.

The product should feel calm and dependable during quick daily check-ins and more reflective reviews. Its tone is plain, compassionate, and respectful without becoming cute, mystical, clinical, or alarmist.

## Anti-references

Luteal must not resemble:

- Astrology or horoscope products that imply a biological relationship with celestial events.
- Cute, infantilizing, or gender-stereotyped wellness branding.
- Gamified health products that reward streaks or create guilt around incomplete tracking.
- Sterile clinical software that makes ordinary self-tracking feel like a medical procedure.
- Decorative cosmic interfaces dominated by star fields, neon accents, glassmorphism, or full-spectrum gradients.

No specific reference product is prescribed. Familiar mobile interaction patterns and clarity take priority over imitation.

## Design Principles

1. **Record without diagnosing.** Distinguish user observations, calculations, estimates, and educational context. Never infer or announce a medical condition.
2. **Private until explicitly shared.** Local data is private by default. Duo permissions are granular, understandable, reversible, and visible at the point of sharing.
3. **Design Duo as a first-class product.** Tracker and partner workflows receive equal product attention while respecting their different needs and permissions.
4. **Show uncertainty honestly.** Predictions use ranges, confidence, and clear language. Never present estimated ovulation, fertility, or menstruation dates as certainties.
5. **Let utility lead identity.** Celestial references may provide restrained atmosphere or phase symbolism, but they must never compete with logging, comprehension, or trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the minimum target. The Android implementation must support scalable text, screen readers, logical focus order, reduced motion, minimum 48 dp touch targets, and sufficient contrast. Color must never be the only way to communicate phase, intensity, selection, status, or risk.

Language must remain inclusive of different bodies, identities, relationships, cycle patterns, fertility intentions, and health histories. Tracking must never punish missed days or assume a regular 28-day cycle, ovulation, sexual activity, pregnancy intention, or a gendered partner role.
